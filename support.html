Don't Die: Survival Support

Need help with Don't Die: Survival? This page covers the main support paths for the game.

Common issues

In-app purchases are unavailable

Make sure your device has an internet connection and that the App Store is available in your region. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

I bought an unlock before

Use Restore Purchases from the Store screen to restore eligible non-consumable purchases associated with your Apple ID. Consumable coin purchases are not restorable after the coins have been delivered and used.

My progress or settings changed

Gameplay progress, high scores, coin balances, unlocks, equipped items, and preferences are stored locally on the device. Deleting the app or losing device data may remove locally stored progress unless it is preserved by Apple's device backup or migration features.

The final level seems impossible

Level 10 is intentionally designed as an unwinnable joke level. This is expected behavior and not a bug.

App Store billing or refund questions

Payments, purchase authorization, billing, refunds, and App Store account management are handled by Apple. Use Apple's App Store support options for billing or refund requests.

Contact

For support questions, contact GF Productions through the support contact information associated with the Don't Die: Survival App Store listing.
