Jump. Dodge. Survive.

Don't Die: Survival is a fast single-player skydiving arcade game about timing, reflexes and risk. Dive through chaotic airspace, pull your chute at the right moment, and stick the landing.

Get Support

10 escalating levels

Each level pushes speed, traffic and landing precision further, ending with a deliberately ridiculous final challenge.

Simple, responsive controls

Dive, brake and steer while avoiding birds, balloons, drones, helicopters, airplanes, debris and other skydivers.

Unlock and customize

Earn coins through play and unlock outfits, planes, drop zones and gameplay perks. Optional in-app purchases are also available.

No account. No ads.

Play without registration or sign-in. Don't Die: Survival does not include third-party advertising or multiplayer services.

Available on iPhone and iPad. Internet access may be required for App Store purchases and purchase restoration.
