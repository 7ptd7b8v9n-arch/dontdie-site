Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Don't Die: Survival is developed and published by GF Productions. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

No account required

Don't Die: Survival does not require you to create an account, register, or sign in.

Information stored on your device

The app may store gameplay information locally on your device, including progress, high scores, settings, coin balances, unlocked items, equipped items, and other game preferences. This information is used to operate the game and is not sent to GF Productions as part of an account or analytics system.

In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Billing, payment information, purchase authorization, and App Store account information are handled by Apple. GF Productions does not receive your full payment card information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Don't Die: Survival does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

Network use

The game itself does not require an external account or gameplay server. Internet access may be used by Apple's services for App Store purchases, restoring eligible purchases, receipt or transaction verification, and related platform functions.

Children

Don't Die: Survival is a general-audience arcade game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account, advertising, or analytics system.

Security

Gameplay data stored locally is protected by the security features of iOS and your device. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the Don't Die: Survival support page.
